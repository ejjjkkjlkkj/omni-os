# VoiceCore v5 evaluation gates

## Blind listening
- Randomized A/B and MOS.
- At least 30 independent listeners for a release-grade comparison.
- French native-speaker subset reported separately.
- Do not reveal model names during evaluation.

## Test domains
1. conversational French;
2. long-form narration;
3. screen-reader navigation;
4. Windows and UEFI terminology;
5. file paths and URLs;
6. numbers, dates, hexadecimal and units;
7. questions, exclamations, parentheticals;
8. very fast speech;
9. low-volume and noisy playback conditions.

## Release criteria
- No statistically meaningful regression on intelligibility.
- Preference and MOS results must be reported with confidence intervals.
- Female voice identity must remain stable across long passages.
- No training/evaluation speaker overlap.
- No claim of "best" without direct contemporary blind comparison.
